Brand Values

Why Larutan?

A real fruit experience, frozen into something fun. Rooted in family heritage, crafted for today.

Pillar 01

Real Fruit Proposition

Larutan is centered on a fruit-forward proposition that delivers the honest, vibrant taste of real fruit in every frozen bite.

Rather than relying on artificial confectionery or generic sugary ice candy syrup, Larutan embraces the juicy character of sun-ripened fruits. From Rasila Raja (Alphonso Mango) to Laal Lazzat (Strawberry) and Peru Pataka (Pink Guava), every popsicle feels like biting into frosty fruit refreshment.

Pillar 02

Refreshing Flavours

Bright, exciting, and refreshing flavour experiences crafted for consumer appeal.

Whether you’re craving the royal richness of Rasila Raja, the chatpata punch of Imli Inferno, the native kick of Jamnu Jhatka, or the exotic thrill of Passion Panga, our flavour lineup is designed to energize summer afternoons and family gatherings alike.

Pillar 03

Quality & Consistency

Built on years of dedicated frozen-dessert manufacturing experience.

Quality is not an accident — it comes from decades of understanding how fruit freezes, how texture is perfected, and how batch consistency is maintained. Every popsicle reflects precision, care, and family oversight.

Pillar 04

Experience & Heritage Since 1985

A journey that began with traditional ice candies four decades ago and lives on in Larutan.

The foundation of customer trust, perseverance, and frozen dessert manufacturing began in 1985. Larutan represents the natural evolution of that family journey — honoring its origins while delivering a fresh, modern experience for the next generation.
