Brand Heritage

A Legacy That Became Larutan.

From a family ice-candy business in 1985 to a modern real fruit popsicle brand for a new generation.

Chapter 1 — 1985

Where It Started

Our family's journey in the frozen dessert industry began in 1985, when we started our ice-candy business.

In those formative days, the market was simple, but our family's ambition was clear: bring genuine refreshment to people with honest effort and steadfast craftsmanship.

“It all started with a simple belief: frozen treats should bring pure, authentic joy.”
Chapter 2 — The Early Days

Hard Work & Hands-On Production

Traditional ice-candy making, long working days and hands-on production created the foundation of the business.

In the early days, the business involved traditional methods of making ice candies and working day and night to produce and deliver products across the region. Every single batch was closely monitored, learning the delicate nuances of temperature, freezing, and flavor consistency.

Those long hours built an unmatched foundation of manufacturing experience that modern technology cannot replicate without heart.

Chapter 3 — Decades of Dedication

Growing Through Trust

Years of customer relationships, manufacturing experience and dedication shaped the family's journey.

Over the years, the business grew through:

  • Hard work — relentless daily commitment to craft.
  • Dedication — refusing to cut corners on taste and quality.
  • Manufacturing experience — four decades of understanding frozen desserts.
  • Customer trust — building lasting bonds with generations of families.

That trust became the bedrock upon which our next chapter would be envisioned.

Chapter 4 — Modern Evolution

A New Generation

Today, that family experience has evolved into Larutan – Real Fruit Popsicles, bringing decades of frozen-dessert experience into a new-generation brand.

Larutan bridges Indian fruit culture, summer nostalgia and contemporary design. We reimagined the conventional ice candy into an exciting real fruit popsicle — vibrant, colourful, premium, and burstingly refreshing.

“Different generation. Same dedication.”
